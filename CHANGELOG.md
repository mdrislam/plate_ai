## 0.0.3

* Fixed the scan area: it now matches what the AI can see.
* Redesigned `PlateScanner`: full-bleed preview, dimmed scan area and a result card.
* Added tap to focus, flash, and a clear message with retry when the camera is refused.
* Added `confirmReads`, `minConfidence`, `resolution` and `hint` to `PlateScanner`.
* Moved the label tables to `lib/src`; they are no longer public.

* Added `PlateLanguage` and `PlateRead.text`, to show a plate in English or Bangla.
